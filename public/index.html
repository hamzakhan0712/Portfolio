How I work

Four lines from import this, the Zen of Python, and what they mean in my day-to-day work.

  • Now is better than never.

    I take a project from the first call with the client through design, build and deployment, and keep supporting it after launch.

  • Errors should never pass silently.

    I write tests where they matter most: Playwright for the pages people use, pytest for business logic, and CI to run them.

  • Explicit is better than implicit.

    I release in versions with a changelog, so clients know exactly what changed and can update safely.

  • Readability counts.

    I keep code plain and note down important technical decisions and why, so whoever works on it next doesn't have to guess.
