The problem

An outbound sales team needed one system for its whole sales flow. Lead lists arrive as CSV and Excel files in different layouts, agents work through them by phone, and closed sales end in payments and GST invoices.

Admins, team leaders and agents needed the same tools, but each should only see the leads they are responsible for, and supervisors needed a live view of breaks.

Backend decisions

One set of views, scoped per role
Admins, team leaders and agents use the same pages. The difference is in the queryset: each role's view starts from a filter on the user, so access does not depend on which template a page renders.
Imports that accept messy files
Lead files are read with pandas, and the user maps their columns to the CRM's fields. Rows that match an existing lead are flagged as duplicates instead of being created twice.
A history on every lead
Every change to a lead is recorded, so a team leader can see who changed a status or reassigned a lead, and when.
Attendance from data the app already has
Attendance is worked out from login sessions, so agents have no separate check-in step.
Live updates over WebSockets
A break monitor shows supervisors, in real time, who is on a break. It is pushed over WebSockets with Django Channels rather than polled.
Invoices from recorded payments
Payments are recorded against leads, and GST invoice PDFs are issued from those records.
